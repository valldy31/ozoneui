Everything you need, nothing you don't

One stylesheet and one script give you a complete component system.

Stats, charts, tables, bento grids, pricing, posts, headers and more, each one a single semantic tag.

Paste llms.txt into any assistant. It builds correct pages on the first try, with <oz-renderer> streaming JSON into live UI.

Switch palettes with ?preset=emerald, derive a theme from a brand color or extract one from a logo.

No bundler, no framework lock-in. Custom elements hydrate automatically wherever you include the script.

Perceptually even colors and accessible contrast in both modes, driven by a handful of CSS variables.

Icon-only mobile headers with slide, modal or dropdown menus, plus grids that collapse cleanly.

Up and running in three steps

From empty file to working UI in under a minute.

STEP 1

Add the stylesheet and the script to your page.
STEP 2

Use <oz-*> elements. Attributes replace dozens of utility classes.
STEP 3

Pick a preset or enter a hex color, and the whole UI follows.

 
<link rel="stylesheet" href="ozone.css">
<script type="module" src="ozone.js"></script>

<oz-stat title="MRR" value="$128,400" delta="+32.4%" trend="up"
         sparkline="40,55,48,72,95,128"></oz-stat>
